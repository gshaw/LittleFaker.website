## Features

- Generates lorem ipsum or text that reads like real writing in English, German, French, Japanese or Simplified Chinese
- Fake names, addresses, phone numbers, emails localized for eight countries
- Keyboard shortcut for any generator
- Integrated with Shortcuts and Spotlight on macOS 26
- Needs macOS 15 or later
- All features are free, no ads, supported by tips
