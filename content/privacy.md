## Little Faker Collects Nothing

I don't collect, use, save or have access to any of your personal data. [Little Faker](/) has no account, no server and no network access of its own. Your settings stay on your Mac.

## Tips

Settings has three optional tips. They're in-app purchases through the App Store: Apple handles the payment, and I never see your name or card. Little Faker only remembers, on your Mac, whether you've tipped. A tip unlocks nothing.
